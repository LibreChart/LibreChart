Background

What is Librechart?

  • A free, open-source Electronic Medical Record built specifically for medical mission clinics.
  • It replaces the paper charts and our older system with a single, shared record for every patient.
  • It keeps a patient's identity separate from each visit — so a returning patient's history follows them.
  • Built on Drupal, a mature and secure platform — nothing to install on your device. You just use a web browser.
  • The project lives on GitHub at github.com/LibreChart/LibreChart.
2
Background

Built for the clinic, not the cloud

  • Runs entirely on a small server here at the clinic. Everything stays on-site.
  • No internet required. The whole system works over our local Wi-Fi network, even when the connection to the outside world is down.
  • Your work is shared instantly. When registration checks a patient in, triage sees them immediately on the same network.
  • Role-based — you see the tools for your station, and patient data stays protected. More on roles shortly.

Who does what

Roles & clinic stations

Registration Staff

Check patients in, create or find the patient record, start the visit.

Triage

Record vitals and the reason for the visit; prepare the patient for care.

Lab Technician

Receive lab orders, run tests, and enter results into the visit.

Clinician

Examine the patient, diagnose, and order treatment, labs, or referrals.

Physical Therapist

See patients referred for PT and document the therapy provided.

Pharmacist

Dispense prescribed medications from the clinic's pharmacy inventory.

Teaching Coordinator

Provide patient education before the visit is completed.

Discharge Staff

Confirm the patient has visited every station, give final paperwork, and hand out sunglasses and reading glasses.

Administrator

Manages users, roles, and clinic settings across the whole system.

The patient journey

One visit, station by station

Registration→ Triage→ Lab Orders & Results→ Clinical Evaluation→ Physical Therapy→ Pharmacy Dispensing→ Education→ Discharge→ Complete
Each station can send the visit to the next station when its work is done.
Physical Therapy is only included when the clinician adds a PT referral — otherwise the visit goes straight to the pharmacy.
Discharge is the final stop — staff confirm every station is complete and hand out sunglasses and reading glasses.

At every station

The same simple rhythm

What you'll do

  • Open the patient's current visit.
  • Fill in your station's part of the record.
  • Save your work.
  • Send the visit on to the next station.

Good to know

  • You see the station that's yours; the system guides the order.
  • The whole team shares one live record — no double entry.
  • A visit can be reopened if something needs a correction.
  • When Discharge finishes, the visit is marked Complete.
